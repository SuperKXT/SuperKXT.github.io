Privacy Policy

This application is a personal, single-user configuration of rclone (https://rclone.org),
used only by its owner to access their own Google Drive files.

The application accesses Google Drive data solely to copy, sync and mount the owner's
files on the owner's own computers. Authentication tokens and cached files are stored
only on those computers.

No Google user data is collected by, or sent to, any server operated by the developer.
It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or AI training.
Data is exchanged only between the owner's computers and Google's services.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
